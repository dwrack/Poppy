What we changed (allowed under "modification: allowModificationRedistribution"):
  - Textures downscaled so the file fits iPhone Safari limits:
    everything capped at 1024 px, normal/specular maps at 512 px, thumbnail
    at 256 px. 10.8 MB -> 5.5 MB. Geometry, rig, spring bones, expressions
    and the meta block are untouched. Script: see AVATAR-STEP1.md.
  - At runtime the app recolours skin, hair, eyes and the clothing
    materials, hides the long back hair for some hair styles, and attaches
    its own procedural hair and garment meshes to the bones. The file on
    disk is not altered by any of that.

Use inside Poppyseed Clothes: a private, invite-only closet game for a
family and friends. No money, no strangers, no hateful or antisocial use.
